### Behavioral framework

I started from the Fogg Behavior Model (Motivation × Ability × Prompt) and built a layered system: a map that gives shape to the journey, a streak that gives daily resolution, and gentle nudges that catch users before they fall off.

<Callout variant="insight">
**Design principle:** Make the next action obvious. Make the streak forgiving. Never shame.
