## Executive Summary

Clinical research showed that auditory training is significantly more effective when delivered in a familiar voice, such as a spouse or close family member. However, manually recording personalized content was operationally unscalable.

I led the design of a secure, AI-powered **Invite & Clone** system that enabled patients to generate personalized therapeutic training using cloned voices from loved ones.

The initiative became a primary differentiator for Lace Pro and elevated the platform&apos;s AI credibility in the market.

## The Problem

Training effectiveness improves with familiar voices, but implementing at scale faced critical barriers:

- Manual recording workflows were inefficient and inconsistent
- Clinics could not manage individualized audio libraries at scale
- Scaling across 1,000+ clinics required full automation
- AI voice cloning risked distrust (uncanny valley effect in healthcare)

We needed scalable personalization without sacrificing trust.

## The Strategy

**Core insight:** Emotional familiarity increases engagement and cognitive processing.

**Design objective:** Build a frictionless, ethically transparent AI voice cloning workflow that:

- Feels safe
- Feels simple
- Feels clinically legitimate
- Scales automatically

**Three pillars:**

1. Seamless invitation flow
2. High-quality recording capture
3. Transparent AI voice synthesis onboarding

## The recording experience

The voice donor flow is browser-based and works on any device. Real-time feedback on audio quality, an explicit consent step tied to the patient&apos;s name, and a clear path to remove the recording at any time.

## Key Design Decisions

### Invite &amp; clone workflow

Patient sends a secure invite to a family member. Clear explanation of purpose and usage. Guided script collection. Removes clinic intervention entirely.

### Mobile-first recording UI

Large prompts, environmental guidance (&ldquo;Record in a quiet room&rdquo;), real-time audio quality feedback, minimal jargon. Non-technical users must produce clinically usable samples.

### Transparent AI onboarding: uncanny valley mitigation

Explicitly stated the voice is AI-generated. Preview-before-use. Full user control over activation. **Trust &gt; novelty.** In healthcare, perceived deception destroys adoption.

### Emotional framing

Positioned as connection-enhancing, not gimmicky. Reinforced dignity and familiarity throughout.
