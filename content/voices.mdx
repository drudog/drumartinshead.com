## The Problem

> _TODO — The clinical and behavioral problem._

- Traditional auditory training uses unfamiliar voices, which makes carryover to real life harder.
- Patients drop off because the practice doesn&apos;t feel relevant.
- Voice cloning unlocks personalization at scale, but raises consent, safety, and clinical-validity questions.
