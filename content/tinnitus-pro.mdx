## Executive Summary

Tinnitus affects millions, yet treatment options are fragmented, unclear, and typically require in-clinic intervention. I led the end-to-end design of Tinnitus Pro, an offering which delivers structured, evidence-based tinnitus relief through guided sound therapy and behavioral support.

In an April 2026 first-look analysis, 40% of patients with bothersome tinnitus achieved clinically meaningful improvement — a reduction of more than 7 points on the THI (Tinnitus Handicap Inventory) per the Zeman 2011 standard — with a median reduction of 13 points among those who improved.
