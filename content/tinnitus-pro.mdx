## The Problem

> _TODO — The tinnitus landscape and what current solutions miss._

Tinnitus is poorly served by existing apps. Most either treat it as a sound-masking problem or layer on generic mindfulness content. The evidence base supports a structured combination of approaches — and that structure is what we built around.
